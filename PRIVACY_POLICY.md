# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 7, 2026**

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Kamusi handles your data.

## 1. Data We Collect

- **Account Information**: If you create an account, we collect your username, email, and password (which is hashed and encrypted for your security).
- **Contributions**: We store the words, definitions, and examples you contribute to the lexicon.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect basic analytics (such as which words are searched most often) to improve the dictionary.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To manage your account and user roles.
- To verify the quality of contributions.
- To analyze linguistic trends to improve the Swahili lexicon.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

- **No Sale of Personal Data**: We do not sell your personal email or identity to third parties.
- **Infrastructure**: We use third-party hosting (such as Render) to keep the site running. These providers have their own privacy policies.
- **Open Data**: Please remember that any content you contribute to the dictionary is public and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## 4. Your Rights

You may request to have your account deleted or your personal information updated at any time by contacting the administrator.

## 5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies only to keep you logged into your account.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy as the project grows. Changes will be posted on this page.
